# Seller or Service-provider Onboarding and Eligibility

Starts when a prospective seller or service provider begins an application. Ends when the application is rejected, withdrawn, awaiting information, approved but pending activation, activated with the correct marketplace scope, suspended, or explicitly requires review.

| Core 20 | [CORE_20_SCENARIOS.md](CORE_20_SCENARIOS.md) |

## Included
- applicant, business, owner, category, territory, contact, and eligibility data
- identity, business, tax, payout, licence, certification, and policy evidence
- automated checks, manual review, correction, resubmission, approval, activation, and revalidation
- seller account, roles, listing permission, payout readiness, audit, privacy, retry, recovery, and abuse

## Excluded
- customer registration
- listing creation and publication
- marketplace matching and negotiation
- commission calculation and payout settlement

The five `*_SKILL.md` files are self-contained.
