# Customer Support Ticket Lifecycle

Starts when a customer issue enters an approved support channel. Ends when the issue is rejected, merged, resolved and closed, or reopened with ownership, SLA, customer communication, and promised business actions kept consistent.

## Included
- customer issue intake, validation, acknowledgement, classification, and deduplication
- priority, queue, assignment, ownership transfer, SLA, escalation, and waiting states
- customer-visible messages, internal notes, attachments, resolution, closure, and reopening
- linked refunds or account actions, privacy, retry, recovery, audit, and misuse

## Excluded
- general inbound marketing messages
- product bug development after support handoff
- standalone refund processing
- identity or role administration

The five `*_SKILL.md` files are self-contained.
