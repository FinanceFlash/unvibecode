# Write a Fraud-risk Decision and Step-up Specification

Use application-native terms. Decide:
- Which actions require real-time, asynchronous, or no fraud decision
- Required signals, freshness, missing-value, quality, and trusted-source policy
- Rule, model, feature, threshold, reason-code, and policy versions
- Allow, challenge, hold, block, and case-creation mappings
- Step-up method, attempts, lifetime, action binding, bypass, and recovery
- Fail-open, fail-closed, hold, or manual-review behaviour during dependency failure
- Decision lifetime and when changed actions require rescoring
- Who may override, release, or close a case and what evidence is required
- Customer communication, explanation, appeal, and support policy
- Privacy, fairness, monitoring, drift, probing, rate, and retention controls

Write scope, actors, objects, states, paths, enforcement outcomes, permissions, recovery, privacy, misuse, acceptance criteria, and unanswered decisions.
