# Review Authentication and Recovery Risk

Review identity proof, credential verification, MFA, sessions, token lifecycle, recovery, permissions, rate limits, concurrency, notifications, privacy, audit, and downstream authorization.

Prioritize risks that could cause unauthorized access, account takeover, data exposure, irreversible security-state changes, or denial of legitimate recovery.
