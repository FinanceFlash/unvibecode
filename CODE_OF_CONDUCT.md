# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in UnvibeCode.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not individuals.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different experience levels.
- Protect private, customer, and security-sensitive information.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect project quality and community safety.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, or disruption of project collaboration are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
