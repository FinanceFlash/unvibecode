Workflow 2

card payment authorization and capture via Authorize.Net

What this workflow does

Authorize.Net provider posts card data to the gateway, updates payment status from waiting/input to confirmed or error, and redirects to the success URL on valid input.

Purpose

What it produces

Who relies on it

payer and merchant using Authorize.Net

Why this workflow matters

  • successful approval creates a confirmed payment
  • declined or errored card input surfaces a form error message
  • transaction id is stored for the approved charge
  • caller is redirected to the success URL on valid form submission
