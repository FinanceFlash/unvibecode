Seeing the impact beyond the changed file

Blast-radius recall and developer confidence in a controlled comparison involving 55 full-stack engineers.

Report prepared 21 October 2026 · Study organizer: UnvibeCode · Exercise conducted by a third party during a developer coding workshop

After seeing the impact of UnvibeCode within our own company, our team was curious: how would it help other engineers understand code?

We invited 55 full-stack engineers to complete a code-understanding exercise in a controlled environment. A third party conducted the exercise to reduce our influence on the process, and participants received no incentive to give UnvibeCode a positive rating.

The results surprised us. Engineers identified more of the components affected by a change, completed the tasks faster, and felt more confident in their assessments.

Precision and confidence

The study reported a modest improvement of approximately 3% in precision overall, alongside larger increases in blast-radius recall.

Average confidence rose by 0.75 points on the five-point scale. This captures participants’ confidence in their assessments; confidence is a self-reported outcome distinct from measured correctness.

Research inspiration: understanding from overview to detail

This study draws inspiration from Gao et al., Understanding Codebase like a Professional! Human–AI Collaboration for Code Comprehension (ICPC 2026). The authors investigate how code auditors understand unfamiliar repositories and use those insights to inform CodeMap, an LLM-assisted comprehension system.

Their work describes a progression from a project overview to code structure and business logic, then to local functions and variables. That progression motivates the question explored here: can structured context help developers identify consequences beyond the file directly in front of them?
